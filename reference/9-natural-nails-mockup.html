Why people travel for it

The Russian manicure, done right.

It's a dry manicure — no soaking. An e-file gently lifts the cuticle so colour can be laid right up to the skin. The result is a cleaner edge and wear that stretches past three weeks.

  • Ultra-clean cuticle line with zero flooding
  • Colour applied closer to the base — grows out gracefully
  • Longer-lasting than a standard gel manicure
  • Loved by clients who want their nails to look freshly done for weeks
